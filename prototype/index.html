Questions buyers ask us first

Can’t find yours? Ask a relationship manager.

How do I know the land title is clean?+

Before booking we give you the full document file: CS, SA, RS and BS khatian, mutation records, DCR, the 25-year title chain and the RAJUK approval letter. Your lawyer can verify them at the land office; we’ll arrange copies.

What happens if handover is delayed?+

After a 6-month grace period, we pay monthly rent compensation at the rate written in your agreement until keys are handed over.

Are there hidden charges at handover?+

No. Utility connections, parking and common-area costs are itemised in the price sheet. Only government registration fees and stamp duty are paid separately, and we show you the estimate upfront.

Can I buy from abroad as an NRB?+

Yes. Book by video call, sign digitally, pay through banking channels and appoint a family member by power of attorney for registration. Our NRB desk walks you through each step.

Which banks give loans for your projects?+

Our projects are pre-approved with several partner banks and housing finance companies, so loan processing is faster. We share the current list and rates when you enquire.
